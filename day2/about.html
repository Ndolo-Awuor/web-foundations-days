About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple web app for writing down short notes the moment an idea comes to you. It keeps your thoughts in one tidy list so you can find them again when you need them.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note into the "New note" box on the Home page.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button to save it.
  3. Find your saved note in the "Your notes" list.

Features

  • Add a new note in one step
  • See all your notes in a single list
  • Keep track of how many notes you have

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note you have typed
Tab Move to the next field or button
Shift + Tab Move back to the previous field or button
